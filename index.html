Good questions

Before you invite us over.

Will the system work during a power cut?

Not unless the hub, router, and relevant devices have backup power. HomePilot’s local-first design is for internet outages; it does not replace an electrical backup system.

Can you see my camera footage?

Not by default. CARE monitors equipment telemetry such as hub uptime, device reachability, storage health, and mesh quality—not footage. Any temporary support access is explicit and controlled.

What happens when the internet is down?

Compatible local controls, sensors, schedules, and scenes continue through the hub and Zigbee or Z-Wave mesh. Remote app access, cloud voice control, and cloud notifications may pause until the connection returns.

Do I need a monthly subscription?

No subscription is required to use your equipment or view your footage. CARE is an optional maintenance plan for monitoring, visits, priority response, updates, and tuning.

Are the listed prices final?

They are indicative ranges for a defined property envelope. After the site survey, you receive a fixed scope, clear variations, and an itemised design pack before committing.
