Good questions

Before you invite us over.

Does “off-grid” mean my villa disconnects from mains power?

No. The security layer has dedicated solar and battery power, while mains can remain a secondary charging source. Your villa stays connected; your protection becomes grid-optional.

Can you see my camera footage?

Not by default. CARE monitors equipment telemetry such as camera uptime, disk health, battery charge, and solar yield—not footage. Any temporary support access is explicit and controlled.

What happens when the internet is down?

Local recording, detection, automations, sirens, and access controls continue. Where specified, notifications fail over to 4G or 5G automatically.

Do I need a monthly subscription?

No subscription is required to use your equipment or view your footage. CARE is an optional maintenance plan for monitoring, visits, priority response, updates, and tuning.

Are the listed prices final?

They are indicative ranges for a defined property envelope. After the site survey, you receive a fixed scope, clear variations, and an itemised design pack before committing.
